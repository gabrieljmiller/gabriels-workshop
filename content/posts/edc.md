<H3>Field Notes</H3>
<p>This is my favorite item in my EDC and has been a game changer after replacing my smartphone. I use a pocket notebook for my to-do list, grocery list, random ideas, stuff to google later, and doodles. Anything important gets noted in Obsidian the next time I am at my desk. This helps me be more mindful and focused compared to using my phone for notes.

I also love the Field Notes brand and I am planning to buy all their national parks editions. But eventually I might get into making my own pocket notebooks.</p>

<br>

<H3>AT&T Cingular Flex 2 Smart Flip</H3>
<p> My beloved flip phone. It does great for calling, fine for texting, inconvenient at best for navigation, and hypothetically could even search the internet if I had the patience for it. This phone is what enables me to live my life without being controlled by my smartphone. Since making the switch, my days are fuller and my attention span is longer. </p>
